Changelog

v1.5.1 November 4, 2026

Fixed region-wide subscriptions: running /subscribe with a game and region (no server) could fail without a confirmation, and those subscriptions never sent alerts. They now work reliably, and any affected subscriptions have been repaired, so you do not need to resubscribe. If setup fails partway, the bot now tells you to try again instead of saving a subscription that cannot alert.

v1.5 August 10, 2026

Subscribe to ALL servers: pick a game + region and choose ALL SERVERS in the webhook form (or use /subscribe with a game + region in Discord) to watch an entire region at once. You get one alert when every server in that region is down and one when they all come back up, with no spam for individual servers. Works with both webhook subscriptions and the Discord bot.

v1.4 August 9, 2026

Webhook subscriptions: get server alerts without adding the bot. Paste a Discord webhook URL, pick a game, region, and server, and ServersUp posts a test alert to confirm before saving. Optional role mention for pings.

v1.3.1 July 13, 2026

Website URL: live status and install docs are now at serversup.app.

v1.3 July 13, 2026

Live status on the website: check World of Warcraft and Final Fantasy XIV UP/DOWN on the status page without adding the Discord bot. Pick a game and region to browse every listed server. The page uses the same live data as Discord alerts, so both stay in sync when a server flips.

v1.2.1 June 19, 2026

Faster regional Battle.net polls for World of Warcraft, so UP/DOWN changes can reach Discord sooner.

v1.2 June 4, 2026

FFXIV support. Regions: na, eu, jp, oce. Example label: ffxiv-na-gilgamesh.

v1.1 May 26, 2026

Multi-region WoW. Required region on subscribe, status, and servers.

v1.0.3 May 15, 2026

/subscribe and /unsubscribe now require you to have Manage Channels or Administrator in the server.

v1.0.2 May 15, 2026

/servers and /status. Rate limits on status.

v1.0.1 May 13, 2026

/games command.
