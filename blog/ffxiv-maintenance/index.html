FFXIV Maintenance (FF14): How Long Are Servers Down?

ServersUp team · August 9, 2026 · 4 min read

FFXIV maintenance usually runs a few hours, but there is no fixed weekly schedule: Square Enix announces every window ahead of time on the Lodestone maintenance feed. The fastest way to know whether your world is up right now is the live status board, which tracks NA, EU, JP and OCE worlds together.

How long does FFXIV maintenance last, and how long will servers be down?

Most scheduled maintenance is a few hours, and the announcement names the planned end time up front. Major patches and expansion launches run longer - sometimes the better part of a day - because they bring the whole service down to add content. Emergency maintenance after an outage or a bad hotfix tends to be on the shorter side, and Square Enix usually follows up with a "recovery" post when it is done.

The honest answer to "how long is FFXIV maintenance" is: check the window in the notice, then treat it as a guess. Planned times slip, and an ongoing window can be extended, so how long FFXIV servers will be down is never more than the stated end time at best. If you are planning around a downtime, assume it runs to the end of the window. Square Enix posts any extension or recovery update on the Lodestone.

When is the next FFXIV maintenance?

FFXIV does not do the weekly Tuesday maintenance most MMOs run. Instead, downtime clusters around patch releases, version updates, and account-side services (Mog Station, the Companion app, the Lodestone itself). Patches land on a cadence Square Enix publishes in advance, so the next maintenance is usually announced days before it happens.

If you see "ffxiv maintenance today" trending, it is almost always one of three things: a patch just dropped, there is an emergency maintenance after an incident, or an account service is down and players are confusing it with the game being down. The Lodestone maintenance feed settles which one it is.

Is FFXIV on maintenance right now? How to check if it is up

Check the live status board first: it shows each world as up or down, so you can tell a world-level maintenance from a problem on your end. Then open the Lodestone maintenance feed to see whether a notice covers your region, data center or world. The game client also shows login status at the character select screen, but the board covers every region in one page so you are not opening three tabs during a patch.

What is FFXIV emergency maintenance?

Emergency maintenance is unscheduled downtime Square Enix announces after the fact or with very little warning, usually to fix a problem. It has no advance window, so the Lodestone notice is the place to look for its scope and expected end. If a world drops with no notice yet, a status alert at least tells you it is the server and not your connection.

Where do I see Lodestone maintenance notices?

Official maintenance announcements are listed in the Lodestone maintenance category for every region. The Lodestone describes what is planned; ServersUp reports what actually happened to your world, with a Discord alert on each UP or DOWN change.

Is maintenance the same for every world?

No. Some maintenance covers all worlds in a region, some is scoped to a single data center or world, and some is account-side entirely. A data center or world-level maintenance means characters on those worlds get disconnected and cannot log back in until it ends, while account-side maintenance can run alongside a live game without kicking anyone out.

That is why the live board shows status per world and per region rather than one giant "the game is down" flag. One data center can be patching while another is completely fine, and a world on the same data center can show a different status from its neighbors.

Patch day vs a sudden outage

Patch day you can usually see coming - the notice is up days ahead, you know your static or your roulettes are getting skipped. Sudden outages are the annoying ones: you are mid dungeon or about to log in for a capped tome window and the world just drops. Both feel identical at the login screen, which is why the board and alerts exist.

FAQ

How long does FFXIV maintenance last?

Usually a few hours. Major patches and expansions run longer, emergency maintenance is usually shorter. The announcement on the Lodestone lists the planned end time, so check the notice before you make plans.

When does FFXIV do maintenance?

There is no fixed weekly window. Maintenance happens around patches, updates, and emergencies, and Square Enix announces scheduled windows on the Lodestone in advance. Emergency maintenance can pop up at any time.

Is FFXIV maintenance the same for every world?

Not always. Some maintenance covers all worlds in a region, some is per data center or per world, and some (Mog Station, the Companion app, the Lodestone) is account-side and does not kick you out of the game.
