Is WoW: Forever Down? Server Status, Maintenance & Alerts

ServersUp team · November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

You launch the client, and WoW: Forever will not let you in. Before reinstalling anything, it helps to know what kind of problem you are looking at, because the fix for each is different: wait, check a notice, or check your own setup. ServersUp tracks the live status of every Forever ruleset in the US, EU, KR and TW, so the first question has a quick answer.

Check live WoW: Forever status

How Forever Realms Work

Most players search for Forever "servers" or "realms", which is natural after years of Retail and Classic. Forever is built differently. There is no realm list to browse. Instead, each region runs one world per ruleset: Normal, PvP, Roleplay and Roleplay PvP in the US and EU, and Normal and PvP in Korea and Taiwan.

That structure matters when the game goes offline, because the thing that goes down is a ruleset in a region. An outage can take out the US Normal world while the EU PvP world stays up. Status tracking has to follow the same lines, which is why ServersUp reports each ruleset separately.

Maintenance and Outages Look Different in Forever

The most important fact about Forever right now is that it is new. A game in its first weeks has more problems than an established one, and the downtime shows it. Launch-era servers get emergency maintenance, short notice and unplanned drops far more often than Retail does today, so a login failure is more likely to be a real outage than a one-off.

Maintenance works differently, too. Retail WoW has a predictable rhythm: maintenance every Tuesday morning, with Classic realms usually getting a shorter window. Forever has no official weekly maintenance day. Downtime since launch has arrived on no fixed schedule, as planned maintenance, as short late-night windows, and as emergency shutdowns announced with only minutes of warning. In the first week alone there were scheduled windows, an emergency shutdown, and a short planned maintenance a few days later.

The practical consequence is that the Tuesday-morning habit does not carry over. With Retail, a missing realm on Tuesday is almost certainly maintenance. With Forever, a ruleset can disappear on any day, with or without an announcement, and it is hard to tell from the login screen alone whether you are waiting an hour or several. Blizzard may settle on a regular window as the game matures, but nothing official says so yet.

Skip the Refreshing: Get Alerted When Forever Is Back

When there is no schedule to plan around, the usual response is to keep checking: reload the status page, reopen the launcher, ask in Discord whether anyone else is on. That is a poor use of time, and it is the part ServersUp is built to remove.

ServersUp runs in the background and checks the status Blizzard publishes for each Forever ruleset about once a minute. It records each change between up and down, and when a ruleset you follow changes, it posts a message to your Discord channel. The check happens whether or not you are looking, so you can stop watching. Because alerts follow Blizzard's published status, they can trail a real outage by a few minutes.

Setup takes a minute. Add the ServersUp bot to your Discord server, run /subscribe, and choose wow-forever, then your region, then either one ruleset or all of them. The bot posts when the ruleset goes down and again when it returns, and it can mention a role if you set one, which suits guilds and Roleplay communities that want the whole channel notified. Servers that do not use the bot can use the webhook option on the install page. ServersUp is free, with no subscription.
