How ServersUp works

When a game server goes down, ServersUp alerts only the Discord servers that are watching it.

  1. ServersUp keeps an eye on every game server, around the clock.
  2. Uh oh. Illidan (US) goes down.
  3. ServersUp spots it right away, through the game's own data feed (API).
  4. Only the Discord servers watching Illidan get an alert. Everyone else stays quiet.
  5. Later, Draenor (EU) and Gilgamesh (NA) go down too.
  6. Their watchers are alerted. Watching a whole region? You get one alert when all of it is down.
